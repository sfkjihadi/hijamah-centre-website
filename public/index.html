What is Hijama?+

Hijama, also called cupping, is a traditional practice that uses suction cups on selected areas of the body. Ask the specialist about the planned procedure, risks, and suitability before consenting.

Is Hijama suitable for everyone?+

No. Suitability depends on your health, medicines, age, and other factors. Consult a qualified healthcare professional first, especially if you are pregnant, take blood thinners, have a bleeding disorder, or have an ongoing medical condition. Do not stop prescribed medicines.

How many points will my session include?+

Each package sets an upper limit. The actual number and placement of points depend on individual assessment and suitability. More points are not necessarily better.

Can I book a female specialist?+

Yes, you can request a female specialist. Availability must be confirmed directly with the centre before attending. Female appointments are arranged with privacy in mind.

What should I know about hygiene and risks?+

Ask about infection-control practices, equipment, consent, and aftercare before your session. Possible risks include bruising, skin irritation, bleeding, infection, and faintness. Seek medical advice if you have concerns.

How do I confirm or change my booking?+

Submit the appointment request form or contact +92 305 9799959 on WhatsApp. Your slot is confirmed only after direct contact with the centre. Contact us directly for changes or cancellations.
